About Me

I am a versatile Computer Engineering Technologist with a passion for building, securing, and optimizing interconnected systems. Graduating with a diploma from the Northern Alberta Institute of Technology (NAIT), I have intentionally kept my technical footprint broad rather than narrowing my focus to a single domain.

My expertise bridges the gap between hardware architecture and high-level software development. On any given day, I am equally comfortable designing low-level bare-metal firmware for microcontrollers, engineering custom PCB layouts, configuring containerized environments, or managing local network infrastructure.

As a natural problem solver and a dedicated generalist, I enjoy analyzing complex systems from the hardware level to the software deployment pipeline. I thrive on learning new technologies quickly, finding elegant engineering solutions, and ensuring that physical components and software infrastructure communicate securely and seamlessly.
